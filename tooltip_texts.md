## Hullmod

Enables the onboard processing of certain resources into more compact and useful forms. Unlike that of planetside industries, the machinery is optimized for compactness and power-efficiency and therefore, resources are processed in a way that merely preserves their total value.

Turns 10% of the ship's cargo capacity into resource processing capacity. One unit of cargo is equivalent to one credit's worth processed per day. As the equipment is installed into the ship's cargo spaces, it reduces cargo capacity by 10%. This reduction does not apply to processing capacity. Usage of surplus flux grid power does not confer additional effects to installed equipment.

Capable of processing volatiles into fuel, metal and transplutonics into supplies, raw ores into metal and transplutonics, respectively, and organics into domestic goods only.

-- S-mod penalty --

Permanent integration into ship systems allows for seamless operation of processing machinery at the cost of increased strain on the flux grid. Requires dedicated power conduits that divert power from existing systems.

Processing capacity increase to 15% of ship's cargo capacity. Cargo capacity reduction increase to 20%. Base burn level reduced by 1. Supply consumption increased by 10%.
